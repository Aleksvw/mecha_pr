Overlap & unique strengths (Mapsly vs Zoho)

🟠 Mapsly exclusives

  • Isochrones / drive‑time polygons for website map
  • Multi‑stop, multi‑day optimized routing
  • “Closest available tech” auto‑assignment
  • Draw & assign visual territories on map
  • Live location tracking + check‑in/check‑out

🔵 Zoho CRM exclusives

  • Full customer timeline & service history
  • Automated reminder campaigns (3‑month oil & inspection)
  • Lead to retainer pipeline + deal tracking
  • Invoicing (Zoho Books integration)
  • Granular user permissions for contractors
  • Reporting / retention analytics
📌 Verdict: they complement each other — Zoho = customer truth, Mapsly = field execution machine. Running only Mapsly means losing automated customer nurturing & retention; running only Zoho means losing routing/territory automation. For independent contractor model, both are justified.

Recommended launch configuration (scalable from day 1)

🗺️ Mapsly Pro

$45/user/mo · annual billing

Multi‑user/day routing, territory auto‑assignment, up to 1000 stops/route, no‑code automation. Essential once you bring >=2 techs (phase 2).

Alternative: Essential ($25) for solo phase, upgrade later

📇 Zoho CRM Enterprise

$52/user/mo · owner only

Territory management, Zia AI (churn prediction), blueprint automation, anomaly detection. Contractors use Standard tier ($20). Total ~$92/mo for 3 users.

Critical for independent contractor fairness & brand consistency

🧑‍💼 Practical note: Each extra technician costs roughly $45 (Mapsly) + $20 (Zoho Standard) = $65/month. With a $50/month retainer, one additional retainer client covers the software cost for that tech. The rest is profit while scaling the brand promise.

Strategic core: The real product is certainty

Oil changes are a commodity. The retainer sells:
“I never have to think about maintenance”, “One person to call when it makes a funny sound”, “I won’t get ripped off”.

That promise demands flawless backend orchestration: automated reminders, transparent dispatching, territory fairness, and invoicing that matches expectations. Zoho + Mapsly together make that invisible magic work at scale.

✅ Every additional retainer client covers the software cost for a new contractor after the first month. Efficiency drives growth and protects the brand.
