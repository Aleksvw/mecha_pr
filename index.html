Why Mapsly + Zoho CRM? (not just one or the other)

Mapsly geospatial layer

  • Isochrones / isocircles → show service area on website
  • Multi-user routing & territory drawing
  • Auto-match closest available technician
  • Travel-aware calendar & check-in/out
  • Real-time location tracking for independent contractors
Required for dispatching 2+ contractors
Isochrones: drive-time polygons, realistic ETAs + territory planning

Zoho CRM customer brain

  • Full service history, interaction log, notes
  • Territory management (Enterprise tier)
  • Lead pipeline (website form → qualified → retainer)
  • Automated 3-month reminders & follow-ups
  • Customer portal & advanced permissions
Trust, retention & scalability
Zoho dashboard: retention metrics, retainer health, service history
Integration fact: Mapsly natively syncs bi-directionally with Zoho CRM (real-time). Territories drawn in Mapsly reflect back to Zoho records. They are designed to work together — not overlap but force multiplier.

Why upgrade to both in Phase 2? The moment you add a second independent contractor, territory conflicts, route inefficiencies, and customer ownership confusion appear. Mapsly Pro + Zoho Enterprise eliminate that friction. Phase 1 can be lightweight, but Phase 2 must include both to protect the brand promise — "one call, no nonsense."

Per-contractor software cost to add: $45 (Mapsly Pro) + $20 (Zoho CRM Standard) = $65/month for each additional technician beyond the owner.

Overlap & unique strengths (Mapsly vs Zoho)

Mapsly exclusives

  • Isochrones / drive-time polygons for website map
  • Multi-stop, multi-day optimized routing
  • "Closest available tech" auto-assignment
  • Draw & assign visual territories on map
  • Live location tracking + check-in/check-out

Zoho CRM exclusives

  • Full customer timeline & service history
  • Automated reminder campaigns (3-month oil & inspection)
  • Lead to retainer pipeline + deal tracking
  • Invoicing (Zoho Books integration)
  • Granular user permissions for contractors
  • Reporting / retention analytics
Verdict: they complement each other — Zoho = customer truth, Mapsly = field execution engine. Running only Mapsly means losing automated customer nurturing & retention; running only Zoho means losing routing/territory automation. For an independent contractor model, both are justified.

Strategic core: The real product is certainty

Oil changes are a commodity. The retainer sells:
"I never have to think about maintenance," "One person to call when it makes a funny sound," "I won't get ripped off."

That promise demands flawless backend orchestration: automated reminders, transparent dispatching, territory fairness, and invoicing that matches expectations. Zoho + Mapsly together make that invisible magic work at scale — and the software cost per additional contractor is predictable and transparent.
